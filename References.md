## Recommended Citation Format (APA Style)

When citing in your report, use the format:

- For books: Author, A. A. (Year). *Book Title* (Edition). Publisher.
- For conference papers: Author, A. A. (Year). Title. In *Conference Name* (pp. Pages). Publisher.
